# Map reference — 7.41f

Compiled by the course author from the current Liquipedia map and neutral
buildings pages, and from the author's own play. **This file is the only
source the diagrams draw the map from.** An agent's recollection of this map
predates the 7.33 expansion and is wrong; two figures in a widely cited 7.33
article are already out of date against the pages below (it says eight
Watchers and puts the Tormentors in the jungles).

Nothing here is a licence to use Valve's artwork. The game map render is
Valve's asset, hosted under game-content terms — it is reference to draw
from, never an asset to ship. This site deploys under CC BY-NC-SA, so every
visual on it is drawn in-house.

## Orientation

A roughly square board, read with **Radiant at the bottom-left (south-west)**
and **Dire at the top-right (north-east)**. The river runs across the
diagonal between them. Radiant's ground is lush and green; Dire's is dark and
red — the two halves do not look alike, and neither are they mirror images in
layout.

The board expanded outward on all four sides in 7.33 and has stayed large:
**a great deal of the map is ground outside the three lanes.** A diagram that
shows only the lane triangle is drawing the pre-7.33 map.

## The three lanes

Unchanged in number and route. Mid is the short diagonal; the top and bottom
lanes are the long way round, running along two edges each.

Each team's **safe lane is the other team's off lane** — bottom is Radiant's
safe and Dire's off; top is Dire's safe and Radiant's off.

## Where the creep waves meet — the fact week 3 turns on

- **Mid:** the waves meet at the centre of the map.
- **Safe lane:** the meeting point sits **closer to the safe-lane team's own
  tier-1 tower**. Both sides' safe lanes behave this way.
- **Off lane:** therefore the reverse. The off-laner meets the wave far from
  their own tower and close to the enemy's.

This asymmetry is the structural reason the off lane is the hard lane, and it
is what week 3's equilibrium diagram has to show: the neutral position of a
wave is **not** the middle of the lane, and it is not the same for both
players standing in it.

(7.41's notes record the meeting point being adjusted toward the off lane,
with temporary speed modifications. That reduces the asymmetry; it does not
remove it. Do not put a figure on it.)

## Neutral buildings

All are permanently invulnerable and visible through fog to both teams.

**Roshan pits — 2.** North-west corner and south-east corner. Roshan moves
between them. **7.41 switched the pit preference to the top pit** — name the
patch anywhere this is stated.

**Twin Gates — 2.** At the north-west and south-east corners, beside the
Roshan pits. Channel one to warp to the other. Neutral: either team may use
either gate.

**Outposts — 4.** Two inside the teams' primary jungles, near the bounty rune
spots; two beyond the safe lanes toward the map edge, near the tier-1 towers.
They start out belonging to the team on their side. The jungle pair can be
captured by the enemy after any tier-2 tower falls; **the pair at the map
edge cannot be captured at all.** Captured outposts can be teleported to.

**Watchers — 10.** All start neutral and capturable; a captured one gives
that team vision for a while, then goes neutral again.
- on the ramp to the north-west Roshan pit
- on the ramp to the south-east Roshan pit
- in front of the Tormentor on the west map border
- in front of the Tormentor on the east map border
- by the outpost in Radiant's secondary jungle
- by the outpost in Dire's secondary jungle
- at the west ramp in Radiant's primary jungle
- at the west ramp in Dire's primary jungle
- in the outer jungle near Radiant's tier-2 tower
- in the outer jungle near Dire's tier-2 tower

**Tormentors — 2.** On the **west and east map borders**, each with a Watcher
in front of it. **7.41 moved the Tormentor spawn to the bottom chasm** — name
the patch wherever this is stated, and treat the two facts as needing the
author's confirmation together, since they may describe different things.

**Lotus Pools — 2.** At the west and east edges of the map, toward the north
and south corners respectively. Neutral; either team may pluck.

**Shrines of Wisdom — 2.** At the west and east edges, closer to the bases,
a little further along the road from the Defender's Gates. 7.41 changed their
experience.
