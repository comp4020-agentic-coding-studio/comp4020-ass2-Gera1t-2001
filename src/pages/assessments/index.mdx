Four assignments, no exam. Each one asks you to do something and then explain
why you did it that way, because the explaining is where most of the learning
turns out to be.

The weights rise across the semester. That is not a difficulty curve so much
as a dependency one: each assignment assumes the habit the previous one built,
and by the last one you are being asked to coordinate four other people who
are all making the decisions you spent the first eleven weeks learning to make
alone.
