This course teaches with thirteen heroes and no more. Dota has well over a
hundred, and a beginner course that tours them learns a hundred names and no
decisions; a fixed pool means you meet the same faces from week to week and
each reappearance is a comparison rather than an introduction. Each week
teaches at most two abilities, always the ones that answer that week's
decision — the full kit is on the official page, linked below, because
reproducing it here would turn a course into an encyclopaedia and Assignment 3
is where you write the encyclopaedia entry yourself.
