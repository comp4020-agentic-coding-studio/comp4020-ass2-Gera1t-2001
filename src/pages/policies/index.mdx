# Policies and support

## Teams and captains

Teams for the final assignment are five, self-formed, and choose their own
captain; where the cohort is not a multiple of ten the tutors fill the empty
seats so every team fields five and every team has an opponent.

## The Hall of Fame carries no marks

The most valuable player from each winning side is listed on the Hall of Fame
page, which is a wall of names and nothing else — it carries no marks, no
bonus and no weight in any assignment.

## The patch is pinned to 7.41f

Every figure on this site comes from patch 7.41f, and a patch released
mid-semester does not change any marking criterion; where the live game and
this site disagree, the assignment you are being marked against is the one
described here.
