# A review is not a story

---

## Decision points

Not a narrative of the game.

A few moments, each answered the same way:

**What did you know? What did you choose? What would you choose now?**

# Reading a patch note

---

## Figures move. Decisions sometimes do.

This course is pinned to 7.41f. The game will not stay there.

What you learned was never the numbers.

It was how to tell a change that moves a **decision** from one that only
moves a **figure**.

Most move a figure.

---

## The five resources, one last time

**Gold. Experience. Time. Space. Information.**

In week 1 it was a list of ours.

Now every cell is filled with your own examples from twelve weeks.

---

## The habit that outlasts the course

Review.

It is the only thing in this course that keeps working after the course ends.
