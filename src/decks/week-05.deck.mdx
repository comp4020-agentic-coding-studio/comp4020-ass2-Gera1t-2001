# Who should I pick?

Week 5

---

## Why it waited until now

Picking a hero is the first decision you make — before the game even starts.

This course held it back for a month on purpose.

You have been learning what a hero does with gold.
This week: what a hero turns gold **into**.

---

## The habit this week breaks

Picking on how a hero looks —

and finding out mid-game that every ability costs mana, and you are a
strength hero.

---

{/* _class: impact */}

# Four attributes, four exchange rates

---

## Strength. Agility. Intelligence. Universal.

Not flavour categories.

Your primary attribute decides what each level — and each item — actually
gives you.

---

## What each one buys

![Four primary attributes on the left, mapped to what each buys on the right. Strength: maximum health and regeneration. Agility: armour and attack speed. Intelligence: maximum mana, mana regeneration and magic resistance — not spell damage. Universal: attack damage from every point of any attribute. A dagger on strength, agility and intelligence marks that your primary attribute also adds attack damage.](../assets/diagrams/attributes.svg)

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> Intelligence does not buy spell damage.

It never has. It is the thing almost everyone assumes.

---

## The line this week turns on

A point of your **primary** attribute also adds attack damage.

A point of any other attribute does not.

A **universal** hero gets a smaller bonus — from every point of every
attribute.

# Same item, different purchase

---

## Two heroes buy the same item

It is worth different amounts to each of them.

That is not a quirk of the item.

It is the attribute system, showing its face in the shop.

---

## Gold's exchange rate

The same gold pays different amounts to different heroes.

The primary attribute sets the rate.

Week 7 is where you start spending on purpose. This week, just see that the
rate exists.

---

## A smaller question in 7.41f

Facets are gone.

Picking a hero is now attributes and abilities — not branches.

---
