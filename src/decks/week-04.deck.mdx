# When should I leave the lane?

Week 4

---

## The first honest "it depends"

The lane stops being the only place worth being.

Pulling, stacking, the camps between the lanes — each is time spent somewhere
other than where the experience is.

Each is a small bet that what comes back is worth what you gave up.

---

## The habit this week breaks

Leaving the lane "to have a look".

Beginners do not fail by being afraid to leave.

They fail by leaving **without a reason** — trading certain experience for
nothing at all.

# The jungle runs on the clock

---

## Camps respawn on the match clock

Not on a timer that starts when you cleared the camp.

On the game clock — at the same moments for everybody in the match.

And only if the camp is empty.

So *is there anything in the jungle right now* is a question with an answer.

---

## Pulling

Draw neutrals into your creep wave, so they clear it for you — or so you take
creeps and neutrals at once.

It is not a general technique. Lane creeps ignore most camps.

It works from a few specific camps only; the lecture page names them.

---

## Stacking

Pull a camp out just before its spawn check.

The camp reads as empty, and a second group spawns beside the first.

No risk in it at all. Only attention.

---

## Blocking

A ward placed where a camp spawns stops it from spawning.

That is half of what a ward does.

The other half — watching people — is week 9.

---

## The courier

It separates *I need this item* from *I must go home*.

That is what makes staying in lane a decision rather than an endurance test.

# Time traded for experience

---

## Name what you went to get

Leaving means giving up a certain share of experience for a possibly bigger
return: jungle gold, a rotation, a camp you stacked earlier.

The trade is only good when you can say it in one line:

**"I gave up X to get Y."**

---

## This week's hero

**Lion** — *Earth Spike*

The most common payoff for leaving a lane is catching someone standing in the
wrong place. Earth Spike is the plainest tool for it.

A support leaving lane is not giving up farm.
They are going to earn it somewhere else.

---

## This week's items

**Observer Ward. Sentry Ward.**

This week a ward blocks a camp. That is all.

Using wards to see people is week 9.
