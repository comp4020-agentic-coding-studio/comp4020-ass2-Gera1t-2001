Teamfights come last rather than first, and this is the clearest place the
course disagrees with the way Dota is normally taught. A fight is the most
legible thing in a match and the worst place to begin, because nearly
everything that decides one was settled earlier — by the draft, by who had
vision, by who could not be prevented from acting. This week also teaches
Captains Mode drafting, because the final assignment is a drafted series and
the draft is where "who goes first" is actually answered.
