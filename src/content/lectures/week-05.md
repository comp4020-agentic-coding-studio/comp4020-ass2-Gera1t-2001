## This week's decision

Picking a hero is the first decision you make before a game even starts, and
this course delays it until now on purpose. For the first month you have been
learning what a hero does with gold. The attribute system is the answer to
what a hero turns gold *into*, and the same purchase buys different things
depending on who is making it.

The mistake this week fixes is picking on how a hero looks, and discovering in
the game that every ability costs mana and you are a strength hero.

## Mechanics

**The four primary attributes.** Strength, agility, intelligence and
universal. Your primary attribute decides what each level actually gives you.

**How attributes become combat.** Strength buys maximum health and health
regeneration. Agility buys armour and attack speed. Intelligence buys maximum
mana, mana regeneration and magic resistance — **not** spell damage, which is
the thing most people assume it does and the thing it has never done.

**What universal is.** A universal hero turns every point of *any* attribute
into +0.45 attack damage, which is why they scale from items that would do
nothing for a hero with a single primary.

**The line this week turns on.** For a hero whose primary attribute it is,
each point of that attribute *also* adds +1 attack damage. That is the whole
argument: the same item, bought by two heroes, is not the same purchase.

**7.41f removed Facets**, so picking a hero is a smaller question than it used
to be. This week needs attributes and abilities, not branches.

## The resource lens

Attributes are gold's exchange rate. The same item pays different amounts to
different heroes, because the primary attribute sets what your gold turns
into. This is the groundwork for week 7, where you start spending it.

## Before the lecture

Valve's in-game tutorial, section 1 — try a hero.

## This week's hero

Three of them: **Sven** for strength, **Phantom Assassin** for agility and
**Zeus** for intelligence.

**No abilities are taught this week, and that is deliberate.** The subject is
attributes, not kits, and each of these three is a clean sample of one
attribute rather than an interesting hero in its own right. Read the full kits
on the official pages linked from the [hero pool](/heroes/) — the point of
this week is what the three have in common with every other hero of their
attribute, not what makes them individually good.

## This week's item

**The small attribute items**, which is what a beginner actually buys before
anything else exists: **Iron Branch** and **Circlet** give a little of every
attribute, while **Gauntlets of Strength**, **Slippers of Agility** and
**Mantle of Intelligence** give more of one.

Buy the same one on two different heroes and it is worth different amounts to
each. That is not a quirk of the item — it is the mechanic above, showing its
face in the shop for the first time. Week 7 is where you start spending on
purpose; this week you only need to see the exchange rate exist.
