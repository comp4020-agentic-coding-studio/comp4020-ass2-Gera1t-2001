## This week's decision

Picking a hero is the first decision you make before a game even starts, and
this course delays it until now on purpose. For the first month you have been
learning what a hero does with gold. The attribute system is the answer to
what a hero turns gold *into*, and the same purchase buys different things
depending on who is making it.

The mistake this week fixes is picking on how a hero looks, and discovering in
the game that every ability costs mana and you are a strength hero.

## Mechanics

**The four primary attributes.** Strength, agility, intelligence and
universal. Your primary attribute decides what each level actually gives you.

**How attributes become combat.** Strength into health and regeneration,
agility into attack speed and armour, intelligence into mana and spell damage.

**What universal is**, and why it exists at all as a fourth option.

**7.41 removed Facets**, so picking a hero is a smaller question than it used
to be. This week needs attributes and abilities, not branches.

## The resource lens

Attributes are gold's exchange rate. The same item pays different amounts to
different heroes, because the primary attribute sets what your gold turns
into. This is the groundwork for week 7, where you start spending it.

## This week's hero

Three of them: **Sven** for strength, **Phantom Assassin** for agility and
**Zeus** for intelligence.

**No abilities are taught this week, and that is deliberate.** The subject is
attributes, not kits, and each of these three is a clean sample of one
attribute rather than an interesting hero in its own right. Read the full kits
on the official pages linked from the [hero pool](/heroes/) — the point of
this week is what the three have in common with every other hero of their
attribute, not what makes them individually good.
