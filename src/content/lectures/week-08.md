Every week so far has been about acquiring something. This one is about the
fact that a hero who cannot act is worth nothing regardless of what they
acquired — control is what turns a strong position into a lost fight, and it
is bought and sold like everything else in the game. The question is phrased
negatively on purpose: beginners lose most of their games to fights that were
already decided before they started, and learning to recognise that shape is
worth more right now than learning to win the fights themselves.
