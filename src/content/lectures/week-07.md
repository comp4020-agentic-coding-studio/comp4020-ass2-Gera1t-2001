Gold is arriving and you have some sense of what your hero wants to become;
the shop is where those two meet. The stance here is deliberately
unfashionable — use the recommended build first, and do not start improvising
until you can say what each item was for, because an item you cannot justify
is worse than one somebody else chose for you. It comes after roles because
what to buy is downstream of what you are for: the same item is correct on one
position and an expensive mistake on another.
