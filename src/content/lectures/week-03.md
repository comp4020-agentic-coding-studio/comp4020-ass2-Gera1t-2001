Last week you learned to take gold off a creep. This week is about the fact
that the place where you do it will not hold still: a wave has a balance
point, that point drifts, and both sides can move it deliberately — so the
ground you are standing on was chosen, either by you or for you. It follows
week 2 because the technique has to exist before the positioning question gets
interesting; there is no point asking where to stand until standing in the
right place buys you something.
