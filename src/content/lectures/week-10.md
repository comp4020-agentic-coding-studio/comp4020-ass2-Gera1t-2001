There is a point in most games where you are clearly winning and nothing is
happening, and this week explains it. Killing heroes feels like progress and
mostly is not: buildings, Roshan and the map are what a lead converts into,
and a team that cannot make the conversion will win every fight and still lose
the game. It is a mid-game week because the question only exists once there is
a lead to convert, which is what the first nine weeks were quietly building.
