## This week's decision

There is a point in most games where you are clearly winning and nothing is
happening. This week explains it. Killing heroes feels like progress and
mostly is not: buildings, Roshan and the map itself are what a lead converts
into, and a team that cannot make that conversion will win every fight and
still lose the game.

The mistake this week fixes is going home to shop after winning a teamfight.
That is the most expensive minute in the game, and it was spent in a menu.

## Mechanics

**A tower is not only an obstacle.** It gives vision, a safe area and gold,
and destroying it permanently changes the shape of the map you are playing on.

**Barracks.** Destroying them permanently weakens the enemy's creep waves —
one of the very few irreversible gains available in this game.

**Roshan and the Aegis.** One extra life, held in reserve. **In 7.41f Roshan's
pit preference moved to the top pit**, so where this fight happens is not
where an older guide will tell you it happens.

**Outposts and runes.** Small, scheduled resources whose value is precisely
that they are scheduled, so you can plan time around them. **7.41f also moved
Tormentor to the bottom chasm and changed the Wisdom Shrine experience.**

**Teleport defence.** One player carrying a scroll is one more defender for
the whole team, which makes a cheap item into a structural one.

## The resource lens

Converting a lead into map. The first nine weeks were about accumulating;
this week is about spending. A team that does not convert an advantage into
buildings watches it expire, because the enemy heroes are growing the whole
time you are admiring yours.

## This week's hero

**Enigma — Black Hole.** It destroys nothing by itself. It creates a window
whose only purpose is to let the team do something else, which is this week's
subject stated as an ability.

## This week's item

**Blink Dagger.** It buys position. Of the five resources, space has been the
most abstract since week 1; the dagger is where it becomes a thing you can pay
for directly.
