Attributes tell you what a hero converts gold into; roles tell you how much
gold a hero is expected to get, and between them they are most of what a draft
argues about. The numbered positions are really a rule for distributing farm
and experience, which is why the role you take changes what you are entitled
to want out of a lane. This week also carries a disagreement the course
declines to resolve: most beginner guides say start on support, a minority say
start in the middle lane, both have a real case, and you will be asked to pick
a side and say why.
