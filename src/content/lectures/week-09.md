The most common sentence in a beginner's game, and it is not really a
complaint about the enemy — it is a report about a resource you chose not to
buy. Vision is the only thing here you spend on purely so that some later
decision will be a better one, which makes it the easiest purchase to skip and
the most expensive to have skipped. It follows control because the two are one
problem seen from either end: last week was about being unable to act, this
week is about not knowing whether you should.
