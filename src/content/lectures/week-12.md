The last week is the assignment: five of you, a drafted series against another
team, and an account afterwards of what decided it. The other half of the week
is about what happens once the course stops — a patch will change the game in
ways that invalidate specific advice and almost never invalidate a way of
deciding, and reading a patch note is largely learning to tell those two
apart. The five resources come back one final time, which is the only sense in
which this week repeats anything.
