## This week's decision

The last week is the assignment: five of you, a drafted series against another
team, and an account afterwards of what decided it.

No hero is named this week either, and for the opposite reason to week 1. Then
it did not matter who you picked. Now this week's heroes are the five your
team drafted, and defending that choice is part of what you hand in.

## Mechanics

**How Assignment 4 runs.** Five-person teams, a tutor as coach, Captains Mode,
best-of-three, and a review afterwards. The details live on the
[Assignment 4 page](/assessments/assignment-4/) and the
[policies page](/policies/); this page only points at them.

**How to write a review.** Not a narrative of the game. Three to five
**decision points**: what you knew, what you chose, and what you would choose
now. That form recovers the whole course, because every week was one decision.

**How to read a patch note.** Versions move and this course is pinned to
7.41f. What you are learning is not the numbers but how to tell which changes
move a *decision* and which only move a figure. Most move a figure.

**Review as a habit.** It is the only thing on this page that keeps working
after the course ends.
