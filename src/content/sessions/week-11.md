## What to bring

Your Assignment 4 team, formed this week.

## How it runs

A Captains Mode draft, **drafted but not played**. Two teams facing each
other, every ban and every pick justified aloud. Run it twice, swapping
captains, so the job is not one person's for the whole semester.

The exact order of the ban and pick phases is confirmed when the course's
facts file lands; the drill works from whatever the client presents on the
day.

## How you know it went well

Every ban has a reason that names something specific you could not beat, and
your team agrees on when in a game it expects to be strongest.

Three separate things land in this week, and they are worth keeping apart:
[Assignment 3](/assessments/assignment-3/) is due at noon tomorrow; Assignment
4 teams form this week; and this is the only draft rehearsal before match day.
