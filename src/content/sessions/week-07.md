## What to bring

A hero you have never played, and the in-game recommended build for it.

## How it runs

One bot match, following the guide **exactly, changing nothing**. Write the
build order down before the game starts. As you buy each item, mark it off and
write one word beside it for what it is for.

Bring the marked-up list to the tutor at the end — the disagreements between
what you wrote and what the tutor would have written are the lesson.

## How you know it went well

You finished the game on an unfamiliar hero without improvising, every item on
your list carries a word for what it does, and you can point to at least one
place you wanted to deviate and say what you would have bought instead.
