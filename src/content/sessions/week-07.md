## What to bring

A hero you have never played, and the in-game recommended build for it.

## How it runs

One bot match, following the guide **exactly, changing nothing**. Before every
purchase, say out loud: "I am buying X because Y."

The tutor listens and only interrupts when a reason plainly does not hold.
Most of them will hold; the useful ones are the purchases where you hear
yourself run out of reason halfway through the sentence.

## How you know it went well

You finished a game on an unfamiliar hero without improvising, and you can
name the moment you wanted to.
