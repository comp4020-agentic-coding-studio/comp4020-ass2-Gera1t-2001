## What to bring

A hero with a hard control spell.

## How it runs

Five versus five, because five is this course's unit and Assignment 4 is
played that way. One rule: **before you use a control spell you must call it
out loud, and having called it you must use it.**

Afterwards, count two things: how many calls you regretted, and how many times
you did not call but afterwards thought you should have. The second number is
the one this week is about.

## How you know it went well

You have both numbers, and at least one specific story about a call that was
wrong and why.
