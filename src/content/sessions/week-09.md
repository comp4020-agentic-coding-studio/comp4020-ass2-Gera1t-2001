## What to bring

Nothing.

## How it runs

Two groups. The defenders get a fixed number of wards and place them; the
attackers start from their base and try to reach a named point without being
seen. Then swap.

The tutor sets the number of wards on the day. Record the run, and review the
defenders' vision together afterwards.

## How you know it went well

On defence you can give a purpose for each ward. On attack you can say which
one you beat and which one caught you, and that is the same knowledge from the
other side.
