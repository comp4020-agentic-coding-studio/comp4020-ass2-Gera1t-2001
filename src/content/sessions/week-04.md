## What to bring

Nothing. The tutor assigns each student a hero from the course pool that can
clear a camp, so the class is not all stacking with the same hero and
comparing identical results.

## How it runs

Practice mode, the whole class starting together. Stack as many camps as you
can inside a fixed window, with the tutor calling the timings out loud.

The second round adds one constraint: keep your lane off your own tower at the
same time. Most people drop one of the two, which is the interesting part.

## How you know it went well

You can say when you pulled, not just that you did — and if you dropped one of
the two tasks in the second round, you know which and why.
