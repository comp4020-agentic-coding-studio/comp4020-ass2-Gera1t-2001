## What to bring

The hero you intend to use for Assignment 1.

## How it runs

In pairs, in practice mode, taking turns. One player takes last hits; the
other **counts out loud**. The counter is learning too — they have to judge
which hits actually counted, which is the same judgement, made about somebody
else. Swap.

The last stretch of the session: the tutor demonstrates the rhythm of denying.

## How you know it went well

You have a number, written down, that somebody else counted. Today's number is
your starting point and not your mark — the
[Assignment 1](/assessments/assignment-1/) threshold is on the assessment
page, and it is due in two weeks.
