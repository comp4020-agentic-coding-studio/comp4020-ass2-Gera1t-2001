## What to bring

The hero you used in week 2. Familiarity is the point — this session is about
the lane, not about learning a kit.

## How it runs

A one-versus-one solo lane in a custom lobby. **The win condition is not
kills. It is which side of the lane the wave is sitting on at the end.** Two
rounds, swapping sides, because the safe lane and the off lane are not the
same job.

The tutor sets the round length at the start.

## How you know it went well

You can point at the moment it got away from you. Everyone loses the wave at
some point in a round; the difference is whether you noticed at the time or
afterwards.
