## What to bring

Nothing. Heroes are assigned.

## How it runs

Each student gets three heroes — Sven, Phantom Assassin and Zeus, one per
primary attribute — and buys **the same item** on all three in practice mode,
comparing what it does each time.

The item is whichever attribute item week 5 settles on when the course's facts
file lands; until then the tutor names it on the day. Then the class pools its
results: what did that item buy on each attribute?

## How you know it went well

You can name which hero the item was worth most on, and the reason is about
the exchange rate rather than about one hero being better.
