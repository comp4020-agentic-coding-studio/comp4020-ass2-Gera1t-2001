## What to bring

A Dota 2 client that actually launches, and a headset with a working
microphone. If either is in doubt, come anyway — sorting that out is half of
what this session is for.

## How it runs

Five-person groups, one bot match each, on Turbo. One rule: **nobody looks at
the scoreboard.** Afterwards each person says one sentence about what they
were doing.

Turbo this week only, and the reason is worth saying: the point is that
everybody finishes a game inside the session. Normal-length matches start in
week 2.

## How you know it went well

You got in, you stayed to the end, and you can name the building that ended
it. Nothing about how you played.
