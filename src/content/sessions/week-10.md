## What to bring

Nothing.

## How it runs

Five-person teams, one custom match. The rule: **after winning a fight,
somebody must call the next objective out loud before the team moves.** A
wrong call is still executed — arguing about it afterwards is cheaper than
standing still.

Afterwards, reconstruct the game from its objective timeline alone: buildings
and Roshan, no kills. A game that cannot be retold that way is usually a game
that was won and not converted.

Note that in 7.41f Roshan's pit preference moved to the top pit and Tormentor
moved to the bottom chasm, so what counts as the available next objective is
not what an older guide will tell you.

## How you know it went well

Your team has a timeline with no kills in it, and it still describes the game.
