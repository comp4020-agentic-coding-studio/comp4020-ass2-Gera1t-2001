She played position five for long enough to stop finding it thankless. The
handle is the joke a support makes about themselves — you buy the wards, you
carry the items, you die first — and it is also this course's argument: the
least visible player on the map is making the most decisions per minute, and
the course is arranged around decisions.

She designed the twelve-week spine and teaches the weeks that frame it: what
the game actually is in week 1, what attributes buy you in week 5, what a role
means in week 6 alongside Nadia, and the information week in week 9. She marks
Assignment 3.
